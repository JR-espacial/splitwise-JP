# Boyo app icon

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool, using the two user-provided photos of Boyo as subject references. Master: `boyo-master.png`. PNG exports in `public/icons/` are resized with macOS `sips`.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE finished square app icon / favicon for a family travel expense app, depicting Boyo, the actual black-and-white dog in the two supplied reference photos (subject reference only). Minimal flat vector-like illustration, bold clean silhouette, no text or letters. Front-facing friendly head only, floppy black ears, mostly black face with an asymmetric broad white central blaze and white muzzle, black nose, warm small amber eyes, tiny cheerful pink tongue. Preserve his recognizable markings, simplify freckles to at most two bold dots. Solid royal blue #2563eb background full bleed, no rounded corners baked in, no shadows, no gradients, no border, no mockup. Face centrally composed within the central 75% of the square, all critical features inside a circle of radius 38% of canvas; generous blue margin around ears and chin for maskable app icon cropping. High contrast white and near-black shapes, readable at 32px, few large shapes, no thin strokes or detailed fur. Reference 1 and 2 show the same dog Boyo. Output 1024x1024.

## Integration

16, 32 and 48 px browser icons; 180 px Apple touch icon; 192 and 512 px PWA icons. Manifest purpose is `any`: this artwork has not been declared maskable, so platforms can preserve it within their icon container. See https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Progressive_web_apps/Manifest/Reference/icons.
